# Tab Bar

<Lead>Switch between the top-level sections of an iPhone or iPad app.</Lead>

<Demo name="tab-bar/floating" platforms={["ios"]} wallpaper height={720} />

## When to use it

Use a tab bar for an app's two to five top-level sections, like Home, Library and Search. Each tab keeps its own place: switching away and back returns to the screen you left.

- Use tabs to navigate, never to run an action. A button that composes or adds belongs in a navigation bar or toolbar.
- Label every tab with a short noun, and pair it with a symbol people know.
- Keep the tab bar visible on every screen of the hierarchy below it. Hide it only for a modal task.
- On macOS, use a [sidebar](/docs/components/sidebar) or [tabs](/docs/components/tabs) instead.

## Usage

```tsx
import { TabBar, TabBarItem, TabBarList, TabBarPanel } from "@dreambase/stardust/tab-bar"
```

`TabBar` holds a `TabBarPanel` per section and the `TabBarList` of items. Give each item and its panel the same `value`, and name the list with `aria-label`. The bar floats over the panels; it sets `--sd-content-inset-bottom` so a [navigation stack](/docs/components/navigation-bar) inside a panel scrolls its last row clear of it.

### Floating

The default is the floating glass capsule of iOS 26. The selected tab takes the accent color, and a glass pill slides to it, with a little give under the finger.

### Classic

`variant="classic"` is the full-width translucent bar of earlier iOS versions, with a hairline along its top. Use it when the app's design follows iOS 18.

<Demo name="tab-bar/classic" platforms={["ios"]} wallpaper height={720} />

### iPad

On iPadOS, the floating tab bar moves to the top of the screen as a compact capsule of labels, centered in the navigation bar's row. The capsule takes the bar's center, so give those screens a large title: an inline title steps out of sight under a top tab bar (it stays the heading for screen readers).

## Platform behavior

- **iOS.** The floating bar is 62 points tall, inset 20 points from the screen's sides and sitting just above the home indicator. Labels are 10-point semibold under 24-point symbols. The classic bar is 49 points plus the home indicator's safe area, with 10-point medium labels.
- **iPadOS.** The floating bar is a 44-point capsule at the top, labels only, centered in the 50-point navigation bar row. The classic bar stays at the bottom, as it was before iPadOS 18.
- **Safe area.** The bar reads `--sd-safe-bottom` (and `--sd-safe-top` on iPad), which default to the device's `env(safe-area-inset-*)` and which a [device frame](/docs/components/device-frame) sets for its screen.
- **macOS.** Mac apps don't use tab bars. Use a sidebar for an app's sections.
- The tab bar has no hover state on touch. With a pointer, unselected tabs dim slightly on hover.

## Accessibility

`TabBar` is built on ui-sdk Tabs, so it follows the WAI-ARIA tabs pattern:

- The list is a `tablist`, each item a `tab` that controls its panel, and each panel is labelled by its tab.
- Tab moves focus into the bar at the selected item. Left and Right Arrow move between items and select them; Home and End go to the first and last.
- Icons sit in `TabsTabIcon`, out of the tab's name, so the label is what's announced.
- Under `prefers-reduced-transparency` the glass turns opaque, and under `prefers-reduced-motion` the pill moves without bouncing.
- In right-to-left layouts the items run right to left, so the first tab sits at the right edge, as on iOS.

### Data attributes and variables

| Attribute or variable | On | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data-variant` | `.sd-tab-bar-list` | `floating` or `classic`. |
| `data-selected` | `.sd-tab-bar-item`, `.sd-tab-bar-panel` | The selected tab and its panel. |
| `--sd-tab-bar-height` | `.sd-tab-bar` | The bar's height, without the safe area. |
| `--sd-tab-bar-margin` | `.sd-tab-bar` | The floating bar's distance from the bottom edge. |
| `--sd-content-inset-bottom` | `.sd-tab-bar` | The space content leaves for the bar. A `NavigationStack` inside a panel pads its end by it. |
| `--sd-safe-top`, `--sd-safe-bottom` | inherited | The device's safe area, from `env()` or a device frame. |
| `--active-tab-left`, `--active-tab-width` | `.sd-tab-bar-indicator` | The selected tab's box, from ui-sdk. |
