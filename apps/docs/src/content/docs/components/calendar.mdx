# Calendar

<Lead>Pick a date or a range from a month that stays on screen.</Lead>

<Demo name="calendar/basic" height={460} />

## When to use it

Use a calendar when the month itself helps: choosing a delivery day, seeing which days are free, picking a reporting period. It stays on the page; for a field that opens a calendar only when asked, use a [Date Picker](/docs/components/date-picker).

Today is marked in accent text. The chosen day fills with the accent. Days that can't be picked are dimmed, and stay focusable so keyboard users hear why.

## Usage

```tsx
import { Calendar } from "@dreambase/stardust/calendar"
```

Values are ISO strings like `"2026-11-20"`. With no children, `Calendar` draws its header and a grid for each month. To change one part, write the parts out: `CalendarHeader`, `CalendarHeading`, `CalendarPrevious`, `CalendarNext` and `CalendarGrid`, which draws its own weekday row and days.

### Ranges and two months

`mode="range"` picks a start and an end. Moving the pointer previews the range, and the days between the two circles join into one continuous band that rounds off where a week wraps. `months={2}` shows two months side by side.

<Demo name="calendar/range" height={420} />

### Compact

`size="sm"` is the dense month of a macOS popover or sidebar, with single-letter weekdays. `unavailable` takes dates, ranges, weekdays or a function.

<Demo name="calendar/compact" />

## Platform behavior

- **macOS.** 13-point days in 26-point circles, a 13-point semibold month title, and small gray chevrons that highlight under the pointer.
- **iPadOS and iOS.** 20-point days in 40-point circles, a 17-point semibold title, uppercase weekday abbreviations, and accent chevrons with 44-point touch targets, as in the iOS graphical date picker.
- **Every platform.** Pressing a day gives a small, instant squeeze. Days from neighboring months are hidden unless you pass `showOutsideDays`.

## Accessibility

- The month is a `grid` named by its title, and the title is announced as it changes.
- Arrow keys move by day and week, Home and End to the week's ends, Page Up and Page Down by month. Enter or Space picks the focused day; Escape cancels an unfinished range.
- Previous and Next are named "Previous month" and "Next month"; pass `aria-label` in another language.

`CalendarHeader` takes the props of a `div`. `CalendarGrid` takes an `offset` for the month it shows. `CalendarDay` draws its number in a circle unless it has children.

### Data attributes

| Attribute | On | Values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data-size` | Calendar | `sm`, `md` |
| `data-selected` | Day | Present on picked days; in a range, every day from start to end |
| `data-today` | Day | Present on today |
| `data-range-start`, `data-range-middle`, `data-range-end` | Day | Where the day sits in a range |
| `data-preview` | Day | Present on days of a range being picked |
| `data-unavailable`, `data-disabled` | Day | Can't be picked |
