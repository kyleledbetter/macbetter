# Sheet

<Lead>Slide a panel in from the edge of the screen, and let people drag it.</Lead>

<Demo name="sheet/detents" platforms={["ios", "ipados", "macos"]} wallpaper height={420} />

## When to use it

Use a bottom sheet on iPhone for supporting content that people look at alongside the page: results on a map, share options, a quick form. With detents, it can rest at half height so the page stays in view, and expand when people need more. Use a side sheet as an inspector on iPad and the Mac, for properties of the selection.

For a task that should take over until it’s done, use a [Dialog](/docs/components/dialog). For a short choice, an [Alert](/docs/components/alert) action sheet.

## Usage

```tsx
import {
  Sheet,
  SheetBody,
  SheetClose,
  SheetContent,
  SheetDescription,
  SheetHeader,
  SheetOutlet,
  SheetTitle,
  SheetTrigger,
} from "@dreambase/stardust/sheet"
```

`SheetContent` brings its backdrop and the grabber. The sheet follows the finger one to one while it’s dragged, and a flick carries momentum: fast enough and it dismisses, or skips a detent. Long content goes in `SheetBody`, which scrolls first and hands the drag to the sheet once it reaches the top.

### Detents

`detents={["medium", "large"]}` opens the sheet at half height and lets people drag it to nearly full height. With more than one detent, the grabber is a button: pressing it steps to the next detent, and arrow keys, Home and End choose one. With a single detent it’s a plain bar. For other resting points, pass ui-sdk’s `snapPoints` (fractions, pixels or `"content"`).

### Inspectors

`side="end"` (or `"start"`) slides a panel in from the trailing edge. On the Mac and iPad it floats inset from the edge with rounded corners. Pass `modal={false}` to keep the page usable beside it.

<Demo name="sheet/inspector" platforms={["macos", "ipados"]} wallpaper height={420} />

### The page behind

Wrap the page in `SheetOutlet` and it recedes as a large sheet rises, scaling down and rounding its corners as iOS does. Its `keyframes` run from closed to fully expanded.

## Platform behavior

- **iOS.** The sheet spans the screen, with 30pt corners and a 36×5pt grabber. The large detent stops just under the status bar. A sheet holding a grouped [List](/docs/components/list) takes the grouped background from edge to edge, as Settings-style sheets do.
- **iPadOS.** A bottom sheet is a centered 620pt card; inspectors float 10pt in from the edge.
- **macOS.** Inspectors float 8pt in from the window edge with 16pt corners; a bottom sheet is a centered 560pt panel.
- A sheet opened from inside another pushes the first one back with the `--sd-spring-sheet` spring. Reduced motion fades sheets in and out instead of sliding them.

## Accessibility

- The content is a `dialog`, named by `SheetTitle` and described by `SheetDescription`. While modal, it keeps Tab inside and makes the page inert.
- Escape and a press on the dimmed page close it, and focus returns to the trigger.
- Opening moves focus to the first control in the content. The grabber, when it’s a button, comes last in the Tab order, is named “Resize sheet” and works with Enter, Space, the arrow keys, Home and End.

`SheetTrigger`, `SheetHeader`, `SheetTitle`, `SheetDescription` and `SheetBody` take their element’s props. `createSheetController` is ui-sdk’s `createDrawerController`.

| Data attribute | On | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data-side` | `.sd-sheet` | The physical edge, with start and end resolved |
| `data-snap-point` | `.sd-sheet` | The detent it rests on |
| `data-expanded` | `.sd-sheet` | Resting at its largest detent |
| `data-dragging` | `.sd-sheet` | Being dragged |
| `data-large` | `.sd-sheet` | Has a large detent, so it's sized to nearly fill the screen |

| CSS variable | Default |
| --- | --- |
| `--sd-sheet-width` | 560px macOS, 620px iPadOS, 440px iOS |
| `--sd-sheet-inspector-width` | 300px macOS, 340px iPadOS |
| `--sd-sheet-top-gap` | The space left above a large sheet |
| `--drawer-progress`, `--drawer-expand`, `--drawer-stack` | Set by ui-sdk while it moves |
