# Generated Image

<Lead>Show one or more generated images on a stage, with thumbnails and actions over the image.</Lead>

<Demo name="ai/generated-image-basic" />

## When to use it

Use a generated image in a reply when the assistant made pictures. A large stage shows one image at a time, the strip of thumbnails lets people compare a set, and the actions over the image let them edit or share the one they like.

- Put the main action, like Edit, at the leading corner and share or save at the trailing corner.
- Name each thumbnail for what it shows when you know it, or "Image 2" when you don't.
- While the image is being made, show the placeholder so the space doesn't jump when it arrives.

## Usage

```tsx
import {
  GeneratedImage,
  GeneratedImageActions,
  GeneratedImageStage,
  GeneratedImageThumbnail,
  GeneratedImageThumbnails,
} from "macbetter/generated-image"
```

`GeneratedImage` holds which image is chosen (`value`, `defaultValue`, `onValueChange`). `GeneratedImageStage` takes any child, like an `<img>`, or a function of the chosen value that returns one. `GeneratedImageThumbnails` holds a `GeneratedImageThumbnail` for each image, with a `value` and an `aria-label`. `GeneratedImageActions` floats its buttons over the stage's bottom corners; use macbetter `Button`s with `variant="glass"`. `aspectRatio` sets the stage's shape.

### One image

Leave out the thumbnails and the stage takes the full width.

<Demo name="ai/generated-image-single" />

### Generating

`state="generating"` shows a slow drift of the theme's colors on the stage, with "Creating image…" shimmering in the middle, and hides the actions. Change the words with `generatingLabel`.

<Demo name="ai/generated-image-generating" />

## Platform behavior

- **macOS.** The thumbnails are a vertical strip on the trailing side of the stage, 56 points square, and scroll when there are more than fit.
- **iOS and iPadOS.** The thumbnails are a row under the stage, 60 points square, and scroll sideways. The image fills the column.
- The chosen thumbnail has an accent ring. The actions are glass buttons, the only blurred surfaces here.
- Under Reduce Motion the placeholder and its shimmer hold still. Under Reduce Transparency the glass buttons turn opaque.

## Accessibility

- The thumbnails are a radio group named "Images", with one tab stop. The arrow keys choose the next or previous image and the stage follows.
- The stage's image needs its own description: give an `<img>` its `alt`, or an Artwork its `title`.
- While generating, the image is marked busy.

### GeneratedImageThumbnails and GeneratedImageThumbnail

`GeneratedImageThumbnails` takes ui-sdk `RadioGroup` props except the value ones, and an `aria-label` (default `"Images"`). `GeneratedImageThumbnail` takes ui-sdk `Radio` props: a `value` and a required `aria-label`. `GeneratedImageActions` renders a `div`.

### Data attributes

| Attribute | Part | Values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data-state` | `GeneratedImage` | `generating`, `ready` |
| `data-checked` | `GeneratedImageThumbnail` | Present on the chosen image |

### CSS variables

| Variable | macOS | iOS | What it sets |

| `--mb-generated-image-max-width` | `520px` | `none` | Stage and strip together |

| `--mb-generated-image-thumbnail-size`, `-thumbnail-radius` | `56px`, `8px` | `60px`, `12px` | A thumbnail |
| `--mb-generated-image-aspect` | set by `aspectRatio` | same | The stage's shape |
