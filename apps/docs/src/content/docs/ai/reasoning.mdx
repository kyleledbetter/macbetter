# Reasoning

<Lead>Show that the model is thinking, then how long it took, with the reasoning a press away.</Lead>

<Demo name="ai/reasoning-basic" />

## When to use it

Use Reasoning at the top of an assistant's turn when the model thinks or works before it answers. While it runs, the line says "Thinking…" with a shimmer, so people know the app hasn't stalled. When it finishes, the line says how long it took, and the reasoning stays one press away for anyone who wants to check it.

- Keep the line short and plain: "Thinking…", "Thought for 12s", "Worked for 2m 1s".
- Collapse it by default once the answer arrives. The answer is what most people came for.
- Put steps the agent took with tools in a [Tool Call](/docs/ai/tool-call) list, not in the reasoning text.

## Usage

```tsx
import { Reasoning, ReasoningContent, ReasoningTrigger, formatDuration } from "macbetter/reasoning"
```

`ReasoningTrigger` writes its own label from the root's `state` and `duration`: "Thinking…" while running, then "Thought for 12s". Pass children to say something else, like "Worked for 2m 1s". `formatDuration(seconds)` is the formatter it uses, exported for your own labels.

### While it runs

`state="running"` shimmers the label and marks the turn busy. `autoCollapse` opens the reasoning while it runs and closes it when `state` turns `done`, so people can watch it stream and then get it out of the way. `glow` adds a soft drift of the theme's colors behind the line.

### Steps instead of text

The content takes anything. A [Plan](/docs/ai/plan) works well for reasoning that came as a list of steps.

## Platform behavior

- **macOS.** 13-point secondary text on one line, with a 9-point chevron. The label turns primary on hover.
- **iOS and iPadOS.** 15-point text, and the line's hit area grows to 44 points.
- The chevron points to the trailing side when closed and turns down when open. In right-to-left layouts it points left and turns the other way.
- The content opens by height with the system's smooth spring. Under Reduce Motion it opens without the slide, and the glow and shimmer hold still.

## Accessibility

- The line is a button that says whether it's expanded. Enter or Space opens and closes it.
- While running, the root is marked busy, and the button's name is "Thinking…".
- The shimmer only ever brightens secondary text, so the label keeps its contrast at every frame.

### Data attributes

| Attribute | Part | Values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data-state` | `Reasoning` | `running`, `done` |
| `data-glow` | `Reasoning` | Present with `glow` |
| `data-open`, `data-closed` | all parts | From ui-sdk Collapsible |
