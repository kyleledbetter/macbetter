# Accessory Tile

<Lead>Show an accessory in the Home grid, switch it from its icon and open its controls from its name.</Lead>

<Demo name="home/accessory-tile-basic" platforms={["ios", "ipados", "macos"]} wallpaper />

## When to use it

Use a tile for each accessory, or each group of accessories, on a Home screen, in a room or in a category. Apple's Home app documents the anatomy: tap the icon on the left side of the tile to turn the accessory on or off, and tap the name on the right side to show its controls.

- Name the tile with the service's name, not the accessory's model or the room: "Pendant lights" in Kitchen, not "Kitchen lights". The HIG's rules for names: letters, numbers, spaces and apostrophes only, starting and ending with a letter or number, and no emoji.
- Say the state in words in the status: "80%", "Off", "Locked", "Heating to 70°". The bright tile shows that it's on, and the words say how.
- Show the room on the tile when it appears outside its room, as in Favorites or a category.
- Don't say "service" or "characteristic" anywhere people can see. They're HomeKit's words for the parts of an accessory, not the Home app's.

## Usage

```tsx
import { AccessoryGrid, AccessoryTile } from "macbetter/accessory-tile"
```

Pass `icon`, `name` and `status`, control `pressed` with `onPressedChange`, and give `onOpen` to open the controls. The tile renders its two buttons for you.

### Sizes

`md` is the wide tile with the name and status. `sm` is the square tile with the icon alone; its name is still read by assistive tech. `lg` is the large square tile, for an accessory you use most. In an `AccessoryGrid`, iPhone has four columns, so a row holds two wide tiles or four square ones.

### States

- `partial`: a group with some members on. Half of the icon's circle fills.
- `state="alert"`: a sensor that detected a leak, smoke or carbon monoxide. The tile turns red and the status is bold; say what happened in words.
- `state="unreachable"`: the accessory isn't responding. The status reads "No Response" unless you say otherwise, and the icon can't be pressed.
- `state="updating"`: a spinner while a change is on its way, like a garage door that's opening.
- `active`: the on look for a tile with nothing to switch, like an open door.

### Controls in a popover or sheet

Build the tile from its parts when the name should open a popover, a sheet or a link. `AccessoryTileDetail` accepts `render`, so it can be any trigger.

## Platform behavior

- **iOS.** Tiles are 18-point continuous rectangles in a four-column grid, as tall as a column is wide. The whole tile gives a little under your finger on press.
- **iPadOS.** The same tiles, in as many columns as fit.
- **macOS.** Smaller tiles with 13-point names. The icon grows slightly under the pointer and dims on press. In Apple's Home app on Mac you also drag tiles to a room in the sidebar and Control-click them to change their size; add a ContextMenu for that.
- **Every platform.** Off is a dim, translucent tile; on is a bright tile with dark text and a tinted icon, in light and dark mode. The tint is darkened where needed so the icon holds 3:1 on the bright tile.

## Accessibility

- The icon is a toggle button named after the accessory, with `aria-pressed`. Space and Enter switch it.
- The name side is a separate button, named by the name and status, so screen readers hear "Pendant lights, 80%". It covers the rest of the tile, so a press anywhere but the icon opens the controls.
- An unreachable tile's toggle stays focusable with `aria-disabled`, so people can still find it and hear "No Response".
- An updating tile sets `aria-busy` and shows a spinner named "Updating".
- State is never color alone: the tile changes brightness and the status says the state in words.

### Parts

| Part | Element | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `AccessoryTileToggle` | ui-sdk `Toggle` | The icon button. Name it with `aria-label`. |
| `AccessoryTileIcon` | `span` | The icon of a tile with nothing to switch. |
| `AccessoryTileDetail` | ui-sdk `Button` | The name side. Takes `render` for a trigger or a link. |
| `AccessoryTileName`, `AccessoryTileRoom`, `AccessoryTileStatus` | `span` | The text inside the detail. |
| `AccessoryGrid` | `div` | The grid of tiles. |

### Data attributes

| Attribute | On | Values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data-tint`, `data-size`, `data-state` | `.mb-accessory-tile` | As the props |
| `data-active`, `data-partial` | `.mb-accessory-tile` | Present when set |
| `data-pressed` | `.mb-accessory-tile-toggle` | Present while on |
