# Energy Readout

<Lead>Show the Grid Forecast: the hours ahead when cleaner electricity is expected.</Lead>

## When to use it

The Home app's Energy category shows a Grid Forecast in which green bars mark when cleaner energy is available, for example from 4 p.m. until 10 p.m. Apps that run thermostats or charge electric vehicles get the same forecast from EnergyKit and can schedule around it.

- Use it to explain a schedule your app chose: "Charging during cleaner hours".
- Say the cleaner hours in a sentence in `summary`; the bars are the picture, not the message.
- EnergyKit's forecast is available only in the contiguous United States. Outside it, don't show an empty forecast.

<Callout type="note" title="The Home app shows the history">
  EnergyKit sends an app's usage to the Home app, which draws the charts, activity logs and monthly trends itself.
  Apple's documentation says apps get these "without building custom statistics or graphing UI". This component
  covers the forecast and a single reading, not a usage history.
</Callout>

## Usage

```tsx
import { EnergyReadout } from "macbetter/energy-readout"
```

Pass one entry per hour with its label and whether it's cleaner, the index of the hour now, and optionally a usage figure.

## Platform behavior

- **iOS and iPadOS.** 56-point bars on a translucent card with 18-point corners.
- **macOS.** 44-point bars and 12-point corners.
- **Every platform.** Cleaner hours are taller as well as green; the hour now has a ring.

## Accessibility

- The forecast is a list with one item per hour, each read as "4 PM: Cleaner" or "2 PM: Less clean, Now".
- The title is a heading, and the summary sentence says the whole forecast at once.
- Height carries the state as well as color.
