## When to use it

Use a camera view wherever a home camera's picture appears: the Cameras row on the Home tab, a camera's full screen, a doorbell notification. The HIG's camera guidance is short:

- **Don't block camera images.** An alert about activity is fine; covering part of the picture isn't. The name, the Live badge and the time sit in the corners, and controls sit on a soft scrim along the bottom edge.
- **Show a microphone button only if the camera supports two-way audio.** HomeKit's camera profile makes every control optional, so check before you show Talk.

The Home app plays live video as soon as a camera opens, offers Live to return from a recorded clip, and can notify people when a camera goes offline.

## Usage

```tsx
import { CameraTile, CameraView } from "macbetter/camera-view"
```

Put the picture inside as `children`: a `<video>` element fed by your app, an `<img>` of the latest snapshot, or any other element. A web page can't open a HomeKit camera stream; a native app or your own camera service provides the picture.

Leave `children` out and the view shows a placeholder: Artwork's `lens` variant, a dim, out-of-focus field seeded by the camera's name and room, so each camera keeps the same one. It doesn't depict a place and it isn't a camera feed; nothing on these pages is.

### States

- `streaming` with `live`: the Live badge, with a red dot and the word.
- `snapshot`: the time of the still, from `timestamp`.
- `connecting`: the picture dims and a spinner sits in the corner.
- `offline`: the picture blurs and the view says "Camera not available".

To show the gray infrared picture a camera switches to in the dark, pass `<Artwork variant="lens" time="night" />` as the picture.

## Platform behavior

- **iOS and iPadOS.** 18-point continuous corners and larger captions. Camera tiles give on press.
- **macOS.** 12-point corners and 12-point captions. A camera tile brightens under the pointer. The Home app on Mac also offers Picture in Picture.
- **Every platform.** Text over the picture is white on a dark gradient in both color schemes, as over video everywhere in Apple's apps.

## Accessibility

- The view is a `figure`. Give it an `aria-label` if the picture needs describing; the placeholder is decorative and hidden from assistive tech.
- The Live badge shows the word as well as the red dot.
- A camera tile is a button named by its camera's name.
- Every control you put in `controls` needs a name, like "Mute" or "Talk".

### CameraScene (deprecated)

`CameraScene` used to draw a porch, a driveway, a kitchen or a backyard. It's kept so existing code still renders, but it now draws the same abstract `lens` placeholder, seeded by its `variant` name. Leave `children` out instead, or pass `<Artwork variant="lens" />` with your own seed.
